# Chunky 32x32

**Tier**: card · **Family**: pixel · **Renderer**: `render.ts` (cell 10, outline)

Backlog row from the 2026-09-05 research pass; a card until the operator keeps it.

## Look

Huge pixels; a character is a dozen cells tall and reads by pose, not by face.

## Palette

Whatever the scene authored, unsnapped; a cell takes the colour that covers it.

## Outline

One cell of dark around the cast, which at this size is a thick contour.

## Lighting

None; there is no room for a highlight in a twelve-cell figure.

## Grid and scale

Cell 10; a reference character is about nine cells tall. Pivot bottom-centre.

## Animation

Two or three frames a clip, each a whole-cell jump; motion comes from big pose changes.

## Best for

Tiny-cast games, icons, a mini-game inside a bigger one.

## Avoid

Faces, fingers, text; nothing under a cell survives.

## Sample

`npm run render:styles` writes `shots/styles/reference--chunky32.png`. Look for four figures you can still tell apart.
