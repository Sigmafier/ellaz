# Rotoscoped pixel

**Tier**: card · **Family**: pixel · **Renderer**: `render.ts` (cell 4, eight muted tones, no outline, no dither)

Backlog row from the 2026-09-05 research pass; a card until the operator keeps it.

## Look

Flat muted tones, no contour, natural proportions: figures that look traced from film.

## Palette

Eight muted colours fixed in `render.ts`, from near-black violet to bone; nothing saturated.

## Outline

None. Silhouette against the ground does the work.

## Lighting

None painted; one tone per surface, as the tracing left it.

## Grid and scale

Cell 4; a reference character is about 22 cells tall. Pivot bottom-centre.

## Animation

Many frames with natural timing; this is the one style where anatomy over pose is right.

## Best for

An adult cinematic platformer, a slow story, anything wanting weight.

## Avoid

Bright cartoon casts; the muted eight flatten them to mud.

## Sample

`npm run render:styles` writes `shots/styles/reference--rotoscope.png`. Look for eight tones and no black edge.
